KindleSociety brings young people together to grow as leaders and to serve their communities through youth leadership, social development and humanitarian work.

Meet the BoardSee our work

A group of friends who decided to act

KindleSociety started in 2023, when a group of friends chose to turn good intentions into steady work for their community.

Today we are a registered non-profit youth organization, formally registered with support from Jaago Foundation. Our belief is simple: give young people real responsibility and they will lead.

How KindleSociety runs

The board makes every decision. Departments and teams carry the work out, and most department heads sit on the board, so plans move quickly.

The board decides

Direction and major programs are set by the Board of Founders.

Departments plan

Each department turns decisions into plans for its area.

Teams deliver

Teams under each department do the work on the ground.
