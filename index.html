A group of friends who decided to act

KindleSociety started in 2023, when a group of friends chose to turn good intentions into steady work for their community.

Today we are a registered non-profit youth organization, registered with the help of Rahman Charitable Trust, a concern of Rahman Group. Our belief is simple: give young people real responsibility and they will lead.

Core Pillars

  • Started: 2023, by a group of friends
  • Status: Registered non-profit youth organization
  • Affiliation: Rahman Charitable Trust (Rahman Group)
  • Focus: Youth leadership, social development, humanitarian work
