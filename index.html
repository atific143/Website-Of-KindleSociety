A group of friends who decided to act

KindleSociety started in 2023, when a group of friends chose to turn good intentions into steady work for their community.

Today we are a registered non-profit youth organization, registered with the help of Rahman Charitable Trust, a concern of Rahman Group. Our belief is simple: give young people real responsibility and they will lead.

How the board is governed

Our Founders Board Charter keeps leadership shared. No single person holds control, and every founder has an equal say.

Annual rotation

The Chairman, Co-Chairman, Secretary and Treasurer posts rotate among the founding members every year, so power is never held by one person for long.

One member, one vote

Whatever their title, every founding member has one equal vote on policy and strategy. Ordinary decisions are made by majority.

Entry barrier

A new founding member needs at least 2 years of active field service and the unanimous approval of the current board.

Three-member veto

Any three founding members can stop or pause a decision that goes against our mission or policy, with a written objection.
